# Product Guidelines

## Guideline Notice

The following product guidelines are soft guidance recommendations for plan design in the Indian market. These guidelines are not enforced by the backend system and are provided to support better plan offerings. Follow them when creating plans to align with market best practices, but deviation is permitted if business requirements differ.

## Popular Validity Periods (Guideline)

Common validity periods in the Indian market are 28 days, 56 days, 84 days, and 365 days. Other effective periods include 30 days, 60 days, 90 days, 180 days. While the system allows any validity from 1-365 days, these popular periods align with typical customer recharge cycles and expectations in India.

## Typical Pricing Bands (Guideline)

For 28-day COMBO plans: ₹199-₹399 range is typical. For 84-day COMBO plans: ₹499-₹999 range is typical. For annual (365-day) COMBO plans: ₹1499-₹3999 range is typical. DATA-only plans are generally priced 20-30% below equivalent COMBO plans. VOICE-only plans are generally priced 40-50% lower than COMBO plans. These bands are guidelines; actual pricing should reflect local competition and target user segments.

## Plan Naming Convention (Guideline)

Use clear, descriptive names for plans when communicating with users. A suggested format: "[Type] [Validity] [Key Benefits] ₹[Price]". Examples: "Combo 28D 2GB/day + Unlimited Calls ₹299", "Data 84D 100GB ₹399", "Voice 365D Unlimited ₹999", "Unlimited 28D 1.5GB FUP ₹449". This naming convention makes it easy for customers to understand plan value quickly.

## Plan Selection Guidance (Guideline)

Recommend DATA or UNLIMITED plans for data-heavy users (streaming, browsing, social media). Recommend COMBO plans for balanced users needing both data and calling. Recommend VOICE plans for users who call frequently but rarely use data. UNLIMITED plans are ideal for users wanting calling without worry, with daily data caps. These recommendations should guide user conversations but are not rigid requirements.
